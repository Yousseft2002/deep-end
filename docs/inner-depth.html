Before you begin

Ask if the other person wants to reflect together. They can skip, stop or correct anything. This is not therapy or a psychological assessment.

Take short notes only with permission. This prototype keeps notes in this page's memory; closing or refreshing it clears them. No AI or network requests are used.

Use a question mark for uncertainty. Don't guess what was meant. Avoid names and identifying details.
