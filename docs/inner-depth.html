Before you begin

Ask if the other person wants to reflect together. They can skip, stop or correct anything. This is not therapy or a psychological assessment.

Take short notes only with permission. Notes stay in this page's memory; closing or refreshing it clears them. Nothing leaves this device unless you both later choose the optional AI organizer, which asks for separate consent.

Optional AI organizer

Send the reviewed notes to an AI?

What it does. A language model reads only the notes you both just reviewed and drafts a set of short reflections in your voice, each tied to the answers it came from. You choose what, if anything, to read aloud. The participant can correct or reject every line.

What is sent. The age group, the reviewed answers, anything volunteered, your marked uncertainties, the optional background and your separately labelled impressions, and communication preferences. Emails, phone numbers, links and long numbers are removed automatically; please check there are no names.

Where it goes. Checking…

What it never does. It does not diagnose, type, or explain causes. It does not keep a history. Nothing is saved on the server, and nothing here is saved on this device.
