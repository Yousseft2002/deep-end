Privacy policy

Effective 27 November 2026

Deep End is a conversation game for two people. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

What stays on your device

The app keeps these things in your phone's local storage, so it can remember them between sessions:

  • which questions you've already seen, per person you play with
  • questions you saved
  • the names you type in (optional)
  • playtest notes and feedback you write
  • your settings

None of this is sent to us or anyone else. We run no server that receives it.

When something leaves your device

Only when you choose to share it. Tapping Send feedback or Share opens your phone's share sheet, and you decide where it goes (a text message or an email, for example). What happens to it next is covered by the app you send it with.

The menu may also offer Report a problem or idea (playtest builds). If you use it, the message you write, the rating and type you pick, and an optional name are sent to the developer's feedback server, together with the screen and depth you were on, who you chose to play with (a category such as "partner" or "friend", never text you typed), whether you were offline, the app version, your browser and device type, your screen size and any error the app hit. Nothing is sent unless you tap Send. The developer stores it to fix problems and may copy the wording into a private issue tracker. To have a report deleted, email the address below.

Inner Depth (optional dictation and AI organizer)

Inner Depth is a guided reflection for two people. Its notes live only in the page's memory and are erased when you end the session, close or refresh the page. By default nothing is sent anywhere.

Adult participants and facilitators may separately agree to optional microphone dictation for a quick note. The browser or device speech-recognition service may transmit audio to its own provider for processing under that provider's privacy policy. Deep End does not record, store or upload audio to its own server; recognized text is placed in the editable note in page memory. You may decline, stop or type instead. Dictation is not offered by Inner Depth for teens or unspecified ages. Device keyboard dictation is governed by the device's own settings.

For adult participants, the facilitator may offer an optional AI organizer. It is only used after both people agree on a separate consent screen and the facilitator enters an access code. If you agree, the reviewed notes (the age group, the answers, anything volunteered, marked uncertainties, optional background, the facilitator's labelled impressions and communication preferences) are sent over an encrypted connection to the developer's server, which forwards them to OpenRouter and from there to the language-model provider that answers. Emails, phone numbers, links and long numbers are removed first; do not include names.

The developer's server keeps nothing: no notes, no generated text, no logs of content. Only event names, error codes, model identifiers, token counts and cost are logged. Requests are restricted to providers that OpenRouter lists as Zero Data Retention endpoints and that do not train on the data; OpenRouter states it does not keep prompts or responses after processing except where law requires. We cannot promise more than the provider verifies; the consent screen shows the current retention statement for the configured setup. The AI organizer is never offered for teenagers or when the age is not specified.

You can decline the AI organizer and use the local organizer instead, and you can end and erase the session at any time, which clears everything on the device.

Third parties

The ordinary conversation game runs without network requests while you play. Optional feedback, Inner Depth dictation and the AI organizer have the separate data flows described above. The app contains its own fonts. The website version is hosted on GitHub Pages; like any web host, GitHub receives your IP address when your browser downloads the app. See the GitHub privacy statement.

Deleting your data

In the app menu, Clear removes your feedback notes and Forget which questions we've seen removes your question history. Deleting the app, or clearing the site's data in your browser, removes everything.

Children

Deep End is not directed at children under 13 and collects no personal information from anyone. Feedback you choose to send is optional; giving a name is too.
